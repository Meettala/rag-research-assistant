Meridian Transit Authority: Passenger Service Policy MTA-2026-04

Meridian Transit Authority is a synthetic organisation created for evaluation purposes only. This policy document is fictional and should not be used as guidance for any real transport service.

Section 1: Scope. This policy applies to all scheduled bus and light rail services operated directly by Meridian Transit Authority. It does not apply to contracted school transport, chartered private hire, or the seasonal harbour ferry, each of which is governed by separate agreements.

Section 2: Delay compensation. Passengers holding a valid single or return ticket may claim compensation when a service arrives at their destination stop more than 20 minutes later than the published timetable. The compensation amount is 50 percent of the single fare paid. Claims must be submitted within 28 days of the affected journey. Claims submitted after 28 days are refused automatically and there is no appeal route for late submission.

Section 3: Season ticket disruption. Holders of a monthly or annual season ticket are covered by a separate arrangement. If scheduled services on a named route are cancelled on more than 6 days within any single calendar month, season ticket holders on that route receive an automatic credit equal to one week of travel. Season ticket holders do not need to submit a claim form; the credit is applied by the Authority within 14 days of month end.

Section 4: Accessibility provision. Every light rail vehicle in the current fleet provides step free boarding from platform level. Bus services operating on routes 12, 30 and 44 use low floor vehicles with a deployable ramp. The Authority commits to publishing a monthly accessibility status report listing any vehicle or stop where step free access is temporarily unavailable.

Section 5: Lost property. Items found on Meridian Transit vehicles are transferred to the central lost property office at Kestrel Street within two working days. Unclaimed items are held for 90 days. Perishable items and items presenting a safety risk are disposed of immediately and are not held. There is no charge to reclaim an item.

Section 6: Complaints. A written complaint receives an acknowledgement within 3 working days and a substantive response within 20 working days. If the passenger remains dissatisfied, the matter may be escalated to the independent Transport Adjudication Panel. The Authority is bound by the Panel's determination.

Section 7: Review. This policy is reviewed annually. The previous version, MTA-2025-11, remains available on request for reference purposes.
