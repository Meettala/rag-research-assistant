# Harborview Library — JR03 unrelated-domain fixture

Harborview Library is a synthetic public-library policy used only for retrieval testing. The main reading room is open from 9 a.m. until 8 p.m. on weekdays and from 10 a.m. until 6 p.m. on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Local residents may register for a standard library card with proof of address. No membership fee is charged to local residents. Replacement cards cost £4, and visitors may purchase a temporary research pass when they do not qualify for local membership.

Borrowed books are normally due after 21 days. Two renewals are permitted when another reader has not reserved the item. Returns can be made at the staffed desk or through the external return slot after closing time.
